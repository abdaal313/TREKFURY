Return Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. 15-Day Return Window

If your gear doesn't fit your needs, you have 15 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. Items must be in their original packaging and in unused, pristine condition.

2. Return Process

To start a return, log into your account, visit your "Order History," and select the items you wish to send back. Our team will provide a prepaid shipping label to your registered email address.

3. Non-Returnable Items

For safety and hygiene, we cannot accept returns on base layers, climbing safety hardware (carabiners, ropes), or gear that shows visible signs of trail wear or damage.

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your item, we will process your refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
