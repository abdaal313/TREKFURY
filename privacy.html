Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Data Collection

At TREKfury, we collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, such as your name, email, and shipping details, to ensure your gear reaches the correct peak.

2. How We Use Information

We use your data to process orders, improve your trekking experience, and send you occasional updates about new gear drops. We never share your data with third-party marketers.

3. Cookies

Our site uses cookies to remember your login session and your cart items. You can disable these in your browser settings, though it may impact your ability to shop efficiently.

4. Security

Your data is protected by 256-bit SSL encryption. We take the security of your expedition data as seriously as we take our own gear testing.
